Built to survive
an inspection, not just a rush.

Most studio software treats consent as an afterthought — a PDF you print, a signature you file in a drawer, a liability waiting to surface the day a client disputes a session or a health board asks questions.

Vaulted was built the other way around. Consent capture, signature verification, and audit history are core infrastructure — the same system handling your bookings is the one protecting your license.

Generic tools bolt on a form field. Vaulted gives every signed form a permanent, tamper-evident record.
